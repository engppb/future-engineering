Models are delivered locally as 1K glTF with their referenced mesh buffers and
1K texture maps. The drill press, portable generator, and crane are imported by
the engineering and operations scenes; the gloves and boots are imported by the
safety scene. The storage cart is currently unused. The garden gloves and rubber
boots are visual references only; they do not imply a safety certification or a
suitability claim for a specific industrial hazard.

No diesel engine, complete conveyor, hard hat, reflective vest, or commercial
vehicle model was available in this verified asset set. Those scene elements
remain procedural or use the existing fleet illustration.
